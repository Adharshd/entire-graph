THE ASSUMPTION THAT WAS ACTUALLY INVALIDATED (verify this, do not take it on trust)
Uncertainty is tiered at the LANGUAGE level (semantic vs inventory-only) but NOT at the EDGE level.
Inside Go these two are rendered identically and treated identically by the classifier:
    IMPORTS os/exec         confidence 1.00   <- a fact
    CALLS   regexp.go       confidence 0.68   <- "matched globally unique method name", a heuristic
A P0 work item can rest entirely on the second, and nothing says so. Confidence is carried through
the whole pipeline and never used to affect a verdict, a priority, an action, or the plan.
